Engineering notes

Short notes on the important choices

A compact record of the decisions that affect stability and reviewability.

Search submits from the input

Pressing Enter on the input avoids autocomplete stealing a page-level keypress.

The selected product is dynamic

The suite picks the first title that matches the search term, then carries that exact title through product and cart checks.

Image checks are practical

The card must have an image. The product page additionally checks that the browser decoded a real image with a positive natural width.

Add-to-cart is the API check

Search and product pages are asserted through UI state. The cart mutation is checked by status, payload, and response.

No random sleeps

Waits are tied to conditions: URL, visible/editable elements, image state, and the cart response.

Reports are refreshed by CI

The latest reports are published on GitHub Pages. Older runs are stored only as timing/history data.
